      How to explore

      Mouse and touch

      Drag to orbit. Scroll or pinch to zoom toward the pointer. Right-drag or two fingers to pan.

      Hover a node to light up what it connects to. Click it to fly there and read more.

      Zoom in and titles turn into descriptions. Bigger nodes are the bigger ideas; brighter links mean closer relevance.
